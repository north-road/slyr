# SLYR Pricing

All SLYR Plugin licenses include:

  • Access to SLYR's full set of tools
  • Perpetual version updates
  • No annual maintenance
  • Full support from North Road

# Costs

# Standard SLYR Plugin License costs

Covers up to 10 users at a single location.

  • Australia/NZ: AU$1950 (+10% GST for Australian customers)
  • USA: USD$1850 (excluding taxes)
  • Rest of the World: €1500 (excluding taxes) This price excludes the VAT. The VAT B2B mechanism will be used on purchases, requiring that the purchasers must account for the tax themselves.

# Multiple Workplace SLYR Plugin License costs

Covers up to 10 users across up to 3 locations.

  • Australia/NZ: AU$3300 (+10% GST for Australian customers)
  • USA: USD$3550 (excluding taxes)
  • Rest of the World: €2650 (excluding taxes) This price excludes the VAT. The VAT B2B mechanism will be used on purchases, requiring that the purchasers must account for the tax themselves.

# Purchase process

  1. Complete the Enquiry Order Form (opens new window)
  2. Pay via Stripe.
  3. Receive your SLYR license and setup instructions.

Note on turnaround times: Orders within our time zone are typically fulfilled the same day. For international purchases (especially in Europe or North America), please allow up to 24 hours due to time differences with our office in Queensland, Australia (AEST, GMT+10).

# Locations and Licenses explained

Licenses are based on the physical location. In the image below, we show an organization with 3 locations (A, B and C) and 7 staff in total spread across those locations. They also have a server centrally location in office A.

The license allows staff to use SLYR:

  • At the designated licensed office
  • In the field
  • Working remotely
  • Traveling for work

There are 3 options available for the situation above:

(1) 1 Standard license per location: Each license can run from a single server located elsewhere
➜ Choose this option if you expect the number of users per office to reach the 10 user threshold

(2) Multiple Workplace license: Maximum 3 locations and/or Maximum 10 users
➜ This would be the recommended option

(3) More than 3 locations/10 users: Contact us or your reseller for a custom quote
➜ Choose this option if there are more than 3 locations OR more than 10 users in total

Every license includes full access to all SLYR features, frequent updates, and personalized support. The only difference is the number of users and locations supported.
